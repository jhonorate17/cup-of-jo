Hello!!! My name is Joel Honorate, but most people call me Jo.

I'm a junior at UNC Asheville, majoring in New Media with a minor in Computer Science.

I study 3D modeling with a dream of working in Game Design. I want to be creating impactful assets, immersive environments, and telling compelling stories.

I was born in Cuba and moved to the US when I was a young lad. When I first started college, I chose business because it felt like the "safe" option. It wasn't for me at all. I ended up leaving school to work in the real world for a while, which gave me the time to be honest with myself about what I actually wanted out of life.

I then found Asheville, and the digital art programs at UNCA. Turns out what I wanted was something like this. Now I'm here, making things I really have a passion for.

If any of it resonates with you or you just want to talk, feel free to reach out! I'm always happy to connect!
