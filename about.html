Hello!!! My name is Joel Honorate, but most people call me Jo.

I'm a junior at UNC Asheville! New Media major with a minor in Computer Science.

I build 3D models and interactive media, which includes props, environments, and digital worlds. Basically all the stuff I grew up obsessing over, except now I make them myself.

I was born in Cuba and moved to the US when I was young. When I started college I went into business because it felt like the responsible choice. It wasn't for me at all. I left, worked other jobs for a good amount of time, and spent a lot of time being honest with myself about what I actually wanted out of life.

I then found Asheville, and the digital art programs at UNCA. Turns out it was this. Now I'm here, building things I actually care about!

If any of it resonates with you or you just want to talk, feel free to reach out! I'm always happy to connect!
